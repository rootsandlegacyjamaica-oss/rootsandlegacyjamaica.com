FAQ

How long does research take?

Most projects run 4–10 weeks depending on scope, record availability, and response times from archives.

Do you work with DNA results?

Yes. We can incorporate your DNA reports to add regional context and help resolve brick‑walls.

Are you a law firm?

No—we provide research & guidance and connect you with licensed attorneys, surveyors, and realtors as needed.
