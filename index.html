Our Story

Bridging generations. Reclaiming roots.

Roots and Legacy Jamaica was founded to help people of Jamaican descent reconnect with their roots in a meaningful, thoughtful way. Born in St. Catherine, with family ties to Westmoreland and Port Royal, our own journey ultimately led us to Portland, Jamaica — where our reconnection truly took shape.

For many, that connection has been distant, unclear, or incomplete. This is your invitation to come back to Jamaica.
